Before you go

Good to know.

  • Home football for both clubs: Dinamo host FC Botoșani on Friday 16 October at 21:00, Rapid host Corvinul Hunedoara on Saturday 17 October at 21:30.
  • Last chances: KINOdiseea, the SevBrick LEGO city and Dinamo’s chess festival all end on Sunday 18 October.
  • Book ahead: Nature Together via apnv.ro (limited spots), Galileo Tehnic via antipa.ro/rezervari (max 15 kids), and the chess festival through the form on its page.
  • Târgul Vrăjitoarelor is free to enter. Rides and workshops are paid with an OCC card you top up at the info points.
  • Veranda Mall double: LEGO city on Level 1 and the chess festival in the same building. Metro to Obor.
