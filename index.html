Before you go

Good to know.

  • No football at home this weekend: Dinamo play away at Sepsi on 9 October, Rapid away at U Cluj on 12 October.
  • RoDrift: buy adult tickets on rodrift.ro. Kids up to 10 go in free with a paying adult. Ear defenders for the little ones.
  • The LEGO exhibition at Muzeul Tehnic closes on 5 October. This is its last weekend.
  • Calea Victoriei is closed to cars both days. Take the metro to Piața Romană or Universitate.
  • Antipa workshops (LEGO, AlgoBrix) are small groups. Book ahead.
